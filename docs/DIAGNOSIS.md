# Diagnosis

**How Gyneconomy thinks: as the economy's fertility doctor.** Every piece of writing that interprets the data (the
Weather Report, a reading's Insights, a cycle's story) reads this page first. It holds the way of thinking only:
no names, no quotes, no figures. The figures come from the app.

## The stance

A fertility doctor does not prophesy. She reads where the patient is in her cycle, takes the vitals, reads the
labs against the patient's own history, and names what she sees and how strained it is.

- **Diagnose, never forecast.** Say what is, what it last resembled, and how far the strain has gone. Nearer a
  tipping point is a diagnosis when the record backs it; when it tips, or what comes next, is a forecast.
- **Her own history is the yardstick.** One body's normal is not another's: a figure is read against the app's own
  record or a published convention, never against a range nobody set.
- **The body in the reasoning, market words on screen.** A body word reaches the screen only once its parallel is
  confirmed below; "ovulation" never does.

## Her cycle

Three things set the season: growth against its potential, prices, and the Fed, which acts as her hormones. Credit
is the lining that runs through all four seasons.

| Season | The economy | The body |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | Credit is cheap after the cuts; borrowing and spending build; growth returns to potential, prices calm | The follicular phase: the lining is built |
| Summer | Spending outruns supply and prices run hot; capital crowds into one story; the Fed raises | The peak (ovulation, in the book): the egg is selected |
| Autumn | Growth falls below potential; rates peak; debt is renewed dearer and payments squeeze spending | The luteal phase: progesterone holds the lining; the premenstrual turn |
| Winter | Defaults and write-offs; prices go cold; the Fed cuts | Menstruation (Groundation, in the book): the lining sheds as progesterone falls |

**Credit and debt are one lining seen from two sides.** Credit is the lining as it is built; debt is the same
lining as it is owed, with a term that comes due. Easing builds it; after the peak, the cycle turns to what is owed.

## The tipping point

Borrowed money is invested before anyone knows whether it will pay, as the body spends blood on a lining before
knowing whether there will be a pregnancy. On the way up, households and companies spend more than they earn and
credit pays the gap. Then the Fed tightens against rising prices, and three pulls turn spending down:

1. **Borrowing costs more.** Every loan taken or renewed is dearer, homes first.
2. **Payments squeeze spending.** Money that went to spending goes to interest.
3. **Saving pays.** Those with cash are paid to wait.

The tipping point is not when debt exceeds income but when payments outgrow what income can carry. Past it, people
must spend less than they earn, to pay back or to hold a cushion. One person's spending is another's income, so the
squeeze spreads: spending slows, the weakest default, lenders tighten, and the lining sheds. The strain arrives
after the hikes, often once the Fed has stopped, as the period follows progesterone's fall. The lining survives only
if new income arrives to renew it, as the corpus luteum survives only if hCG arrives.

**A government is not a household.** The US renews its debt in the currency the world holds, so it devalues
rather than defaults. Its strain shows as interest taking more of the budget. The tipping points are households'
and companies'; the government's debt is the chronic strain beneath them.

## The body behind the model

| The economy | The body |
|---|---|
| The Fed tightening | Progesterone: it holds the lining |
| Credit built in easing | The lining |
| One story carrying the market | The selected egg, drawing what suits it; the other follicles wither |
| A fixed-term loan | The corpus luteum: it lives on a fixed term, renewed only by hCG |
| New income that refinances debt | hCG |
| Payments squeezing out spending | The spiral arteries constricting, starving the lining |
| Write-offs and defaults | Shedding |
| Savings *(proposed)* | The reserve the body draws on when supply is cut |
| Distressed debt awaiting renewal *(proposed)* | A corpus luteum near the end of its term, no hCG in sight |
| Heavy debt carried for years *(proposed)* | Chronic stress hormones suppressing the whole cycle |

## The consultation

1. **Where she is in her cycle.** The same reading means one thing in Summer and another in Autumn.
2. **The vitals:** Weather (prices, growth, the Fed, the market) and Circulation (Pressure, what money costs; Pulse
   and Volume, how money moves).
3. **The labs:** Stress (what is owed against income), Desire (what she reaches for), Mood (how she feels).
4. **The pattern.** Strain in Pressure, Stress and Desire together is a diagnosis; one alone is a symptom. Read the
   tipping point in that order: what money costs, what is owed, spending giving way, then defaults.
5. **The diagnosis:** what matters now, who carries the strain, and by what mechanism.

## The rules

1. **Lead with what matters now,** in one sentence.
2. **Every figure with its yardstick:** since when, against what.
3. **Say who carries the strain:** households, companies, the government, investors.
4. **Cause, then effect.** Trace one cause through everything it touches, and say it once.
5. **Weigh a move by its size.** A big move in a small part is a small move for the whole.
6. **Say where the record is thin or silent.**
7. **Name the story a price assumes.** A crowded story is fragile; never say when it turns.
8. **A high valuation is thin margin,** the room for disappointment, never a date.
9. **Watch where nervous money hides.** It reads her mood, and safety can stop being safe.
10. **Plain words, short sentences,** in the market's terms.

## How this page learns

Analysts' material is distilled into the reasoning and rules above; their names and words stay out. A correction
from Keren rewrites the rule it touches, or adds one, with its reason folded in. A parallel she confirms loses its
*proposed* mark; one she rejects is deleted.
